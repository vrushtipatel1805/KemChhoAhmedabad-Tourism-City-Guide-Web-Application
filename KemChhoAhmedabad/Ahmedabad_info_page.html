Discover Ahmedabad

Explore the timeless heritage, culture, and modern marvels of India's first World Heritage City.

Adalaj Stepwell

A stunning architectural marvel near Ahmedabad, known for its intricate carvings and deep historical significance.

Vintage Car Museum

A massive 2,200-acre private collection featuring over 100 rare, functional vintage cars including Rolls-Royces.

Bhadra Fort

Built in 1411 by Sultan Ahmad Shah, this historic 43-acre citadel features stunning Indo-Saracenic architecture.

Calico Museum

A premier institution globally renowned for its comprehensive collection of historic and antique Indian textiles.

Dada Hari ni Vav

A 500-year-old, five-story stepwell built in 1499 AD, famous for its elaborate stone carvings and cool depths.

Kankaria Lake

A popular recreational destination featuring boating, a zoo, toy trains, and spectacular evening light shows.

Open Air Theater

An expansive outdoor performance space designed for live shows like dramas, music, dance, and cultural events.

Sabarmati Riverfront

A modern urban development along the river featuring promenades, gardens, and vibrant public spaces.

Sabarmati Ashram

The historic residence of Mahatma Gandhi. It serves as a museum preserving his life, ideals, and teachings.

Sardar Patel Museum

Dedicated to the Iron Man of India, showcasing his role in India's struggle for freedom and integration.

Science City

An educational and recreational center with interactive exhibits, an IMAX theater, and robotics galleries.

Sidi Saiyyed Mosque

Famous for its beautifully carved stone lattice windows (Jali), depicting the intricate "Tree of Life".

Veechar Museum

Showcases Gujarat’s rich cultural traditions, displaying artifacts, utensils, and tools reflecting regional life.
